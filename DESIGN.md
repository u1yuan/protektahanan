# Protektahanan interface

Code-first guided flow approved by the user. Globe-inspired blue accents (#0056B8), dark navy text, pale blue backgrounds, and white surfaces. Assessment indicators combine a large symbol, explicit bilingual label, and immediate guidance: red exclamation for strong warning signs, amber question mark for uncertainty, and blue check for no obvious warning signs. The blue check explicitly does not prove safety or authenticity. Large legible type, native controls, restrained separators. A focused 720px column with input, findings, then an optional editable report. Language switches never erase results. No account, dashboard, scores, or alarming verdicts. Keyboard focus follows steps; statuses and errors announce accessibly. Small screens reflow and the text-size control enlarges content.
